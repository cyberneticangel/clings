insert: if root is NULL, malloc a new node (children NULL) and return it.
Otherwise recurse: root->left = tree_insert(root->left, value) for smaller
values, right for larger, and return root.
contains: compare and go left or right until you find it or hit NULL.
height: 0 for NULL, else 1 + the larger of the two children's heights.
in_order: recurse left, then out[(*n)++] = root->value, then recurse right.
free: free both children first, then the node (post-order).
