map_new: malloc the Map, calloc(buckets, sizeof(Entry *)) for the buckets
(calloc gives NULL = empty list in every bucket).
map_get: bucket = hash(key) % bucket_count; walk that list comparing with
strcmp; return &e->value when found.
map_increment: use map_get; if missing, malloc an Entry and a copy of the key
(strlen + 1!), then push it on the front of its bucket's list. size++.
count_words: collect letters (lower-cased) into a small char buffer; when you
hit a non-letter (or the final '\0'), and the buffer isn't empty, terminate
it and map_increment it.
map_free: free every entry's key, then the entry (save next first!), then
the bucket array, then the map.
