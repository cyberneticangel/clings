word_count: track whether you're currently inside a word (a bool). Count
when you go from "not in a word" to a non-space character.
capitalize_words: same idea. A `start` flag that's true after whitespace.
is_palindrome: two indices, i from the front and j from the back. Skip
non-letters on either side; compare tolower() of both; move inward.
Watch out for len == 0 (len - 1 underflows for size_t!).
caesar: normalize the shift into 0..25 with ((shift % 26) + 26) % 26, then
for letters: 'a' + (c - 'a' + shift) % 26 (same with 'A').
