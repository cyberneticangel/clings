Leap: (year % 4 == 0 && year % 100 != 0) || year % 400 == 0
days_in_month: a switch with grouped cases, and `cond ? 29 : 28` for February.
day_of_year: day + days_in_month(m, year) for every month m before `month`.
day_of_week: count days from 1900-01-01 (a Monday, so weekday 1):
add 365/366 for each full year, then day_of_year(...) - 1. The answer is
(1 + days) % 7.
